# Support

- **Judges and testers:** follow [docs/JUDGE_TESTING.md](docs/JUDGE_TESTING.md). The setup page inside the app
  shows a health check for each part (Rovo, workflow validator, MCP access) and what to do when one is red.
- **Bugs:** open an issue with the **Bug** template. Include the site, environment and the work item key.
- **Security issues:** never in a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Questions about why something works the way it does:** the arbiter is [docs/PROJECT.md](docs/PROJECT.md);
  the details are in [docs/specs](docs/specs).
